# ESC Volunteer Hostel Toolkit

Interactive implementation toolkit for founding a volunteer-based social-enterprise hostel through the European Solidarity Corps.
Research output of the M2 MTLCI dissertation *Economic Sustainability in Volunteer-Based Hostels: Designing Viable Operations through the European Solidarity Corps* (EYVAZLI Mahin, KEDGE Business School, 2025–2026, supervisor Jorge Andres Clarke de La Cerda).

- `index.html` — the interactive application (nine phases, six city contexts, two-layer financial model, legal reference).
- `explanatory-document.pdf` — companion document listing every data point, source and status label.

Prototype estimates for academic purposes; not financial or legal advice. Verify every figure with the national ESC agency and local authorities before acting.
